# Open film playback research

**Checked:** 2026-10-28 (Internet Archive metadata, item pages and HTTP headers). This is a bounded source audit, not a legal opinion or a claim of playback on every device/network.

**User steering after the prototype:** the user explicitly wants the configured addons, not an open/public-domain substitute. The optional provider is OFF by default (`FUBOL_OPEN_FILMS=0`) and was disabled in the running container. Its decode job was stopped on that instruction. The research below is retained as evidence, not as fulfillment of the requested addon catalog or a recommendation to replace it.

## Feasible built-in provider

Implement a small, curated Internet Archive provider—not a new “all movies” promise:

1. Keep a short, reviewable seed list of actual feature item identifiers; fetch each item's metadata endpoint and fail closed on missing/ambiguous rights metadata. Surface the rights label/link from the source, without claiming IA's assertion settles jurisdiction or rights in every restoration/transfer.
2. Publish only items with a real full-length MP4 file (`.mp4`, `video/mp4`/IA media metadata) and an explicit supported source rights statement. Prefer h.264 IA MP4 derivatives when originals are an unsupported container; do not infer codecs solely from the extension.
3. Offer captions only when a language-tagged or clearly identified caption asset exists in the **same item** and its relation to the selected encode is established. Label ASR captions as machine-generated; do not market them as perfect or promise universal synchronization. Keep any unverified external/version-mismatched SRT unavailable by default.
4. Build playback URLs from the item identifier plus encoded filename, use browser native `<video controls>` with byte-range streaming, and keep subtitles as a selectable `<track>`/same-origin proxied VTT/SRT conversion if required. Verify range behavior and one real playback path end-to-end before listing an item as playable; report per-title errors honestly.
5. Recheck metadata/availability at runtime or on a short cache expiry. Source rate limits, removals, derivative changes, regional rights, and device codec support can make a previously available item fail; an empty verified catalog is safer than demos or falsely matched substitutes.

**Recommendation:** first use `night_of_the_living_dead_dvd` as a narrowly labeled prototype entry and actually test its opening, seeking, audio, and the ASR SRT in the app. The research establishes a credible asset pair, not synchronization accuracy or full-session acceptance. Keep the other two out of the advertised playable list until their rights/caption gaps are resolved.
